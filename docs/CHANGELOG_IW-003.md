# IW-003 — Inventory Analytics Foundation

- Latest-snapshot inventory analytics report.
- Totals for physical, available, committed, transit, purchase-order and inventory-value measures.
- Available and committed ratios.
- Breakdown by brand, location and product.
- Stock-position classification: available, out of stock, negative, overcommitted, inbound only and no available stock.
