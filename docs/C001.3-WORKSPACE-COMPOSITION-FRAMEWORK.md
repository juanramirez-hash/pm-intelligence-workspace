# C-001.3 — Workspace Composition Framework

Version: `0.22.0-C001.3`

## Objective

Create a reusable composition layer for specialized workspaces and migrate Customer Intelligence to the Atlas executive experience without moving business calculations into React.

## Delivered

- `WorkspaceComposition` reusable shell with explicit slots for breadcrumbs, hero, brief, opportunity, KPIs, directory and panels.
- Customer adapter that translates `CustomerWorkspaceViewModel` into Atlas KPI contracts.
- Customer Intelligence page migrated to Atlas Executive Hero, KPI cards and Executive Panels.
- Customer Health Score, confidence, risk, opportunities, timeline and abandoned products exposed from the existing deterministic Customer Decision Engine.
- Brand-scoped customer analysis preserved through the URL query parameter.
- No dependency on `NormalizedSalesRow[]` from the presentation layer.

## Architecture

```text
BusinessRepository
      ↓
CustomerDecisionEngine
      ↓
CustomerWorkspaceViewModel
      ↓
Customer Atlas Adapter
      ↓
WorkspaceComposition
      ↓
React
```
