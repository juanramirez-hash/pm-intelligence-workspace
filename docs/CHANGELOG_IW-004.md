# IW-004 — Inventory Risk & Opportunity Engine

## Added

- Deterministic inventory risk signals for negative stock, stockouts, overcommitment, no available stock, unresolved product identity, and inventory-value concentration.
- Inventory opportunities for internal transfers, purchase review, inbound follow-up, and commitment review.
- Priority and score model from 0 to 100.
- Repository queries through `repository.inventoryRiskOpportunity`.
- Immutable report access and filters by type and priority.
